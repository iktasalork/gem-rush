How to play

Drag your finger anywhere (or use ← → keys) to slide the catcher.

💎🔷🔶Gems: +10 points ⭐Star: +50 points 💣Bomb: lose a life! ❤️Heart: +1 life (max 5) 🛡️Shield: blocks one bomb 🧲Magnet: pulls goodies in ⏳Slow-mo: everything slows

Missing a gem is fine, but it breaks your combo. Every 5 catches in a row raises your multiplier!
